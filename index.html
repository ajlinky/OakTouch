01 / OUR STORY

Built small.
Built intentionally.

Oak Touch opens every evening in Badore, Ajah — a small food operation built around one simple promise: food is prepared fresh for the day it is sold, with careful attention to hygiene, consistency, and presentation.

What started as a single pot of akara has grown, batch by batch, into a small evening kitchen with a growing walk-up customer base.

FRESH

Fresh sourcing

Ingredients are purchased fresh for each day's production, with no unnecessary stock carried over.

CLEAN

Clean process

Hygienic preparation and a clean close-down routine, every batch, every night.

INTENTIONAL

Small-batch focus

Small-batch cooking with attention to every plate — not mass production.

03 / HOW WE WORK

The nightly rhythm.

2:30 – 3:00 PM

Washing & prep

Ingredients are washed, measured and prepared for the night's batch.

4:00 – 5:00 PM

Production setup

Cooking begins. Umbrella, lighting, gas and water are set up on site.

6:00 PM

Service opens

The evening service begins.

11:30 PM – 12:00 AM

Close & account

Firm closing window. Remaining good-condition food is redirected rather than carried into the next day, and the night's records are closed.
